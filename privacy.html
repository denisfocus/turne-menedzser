Privacy Policy — Tour Empire

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Tour Empire ("the game", "we") is a single-player music tour management simulator. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data is handled when you play, and how.

Data you enter

Your agency name, chosen headquarters and all game progress are stored only on your device (in local storage). We do not upload, read, or have access to your saved game.

Data collected by third parties

Advertising (Google AdMob). When you choose to watch an optional rewarded video, Google's advertising SDK may collect a device identifier and basic ad-interaction data in order to serve and measure the ad. The advertising SDK may also collect anonymous crash and performance (diagnostics) data to keep the ads stable; this is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you. The game requests non-personalized ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases (Apple App Store / Google Play). Optional credit purchases are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment details.

External media. The map, city photos and fonts load from third-party services (map tiles, image and font providers). Loading them uses a standard network request that may expose your IP address to those services, as with any website.

Anonymous game statistics

The game can record anonymous gameplay events (for example "tour finished") to help improve balance. These contain no name, email, or personal identifier. You can turn them off any time in Office → Help → Anonymous game statistics.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information, and we do not sell any data.

Children

The game is rated suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn off anonymous statistics in the game, decline the optional rewarded ads, and delete all local data by removing the app or clearing its storage.
